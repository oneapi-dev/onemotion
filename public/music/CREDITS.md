# Built-in music

All tracks are from [FreePD](https://freepd.com/) and are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; we credit the composers anyway.

FreePD.com (Kevin MacLeod) published these tracks under CC0: free for commercial use, no attribution required, and its FAQ allows including them in an app that generates movies for users. FreePD has closed; the files come from a public mirror and are checked against the SHA-256 recorded here. Descriptions are FreePD's own.

Each file is the first 90 seconds of the original, loudness-normalized and faded out.
